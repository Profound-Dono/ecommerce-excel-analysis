# Business Problem

## Background

The company is an e-commerce business selling products across multiple categories and sales channels.

Management wants to better understand overall sales performance and identify the products and categories that are driving revenue.

## Business Request

The management team requested an analysis of sales performance with a particular focus on:

* Monthly revenue performance
* Product performance
* Category performance
* Customer revenue contribution
* Sales-channel performance

## Key Questions

1. Which customers generate the most revenue?
2. Which products generate the most revenue?
3. How does January revenue compare with average monthly revenue?
4. How does January revenue compare with December?
5. Which product category generates the most revenue?

## Desired Outcome

The goal is to transform the raw sales data into a reliable analytical dataset and an interactive management dashboard that allows decision-makers to quickly identify important trends and areas requiring further investigation.

The analysis should not only report what happened, but also distinguish between observed facts and assumptions about why those changes occurred.
